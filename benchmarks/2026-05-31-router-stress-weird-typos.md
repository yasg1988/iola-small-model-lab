## Critical Findings

The model is not reliable as the only guardrail.

Main failures:

- Unsupported objects are often forced into supported layers. Examples: hospital, market, theater, MFC, hotel, bank, cafe, museum.
- Some impossible entities become invented selectors. Examples: `entity_number: 111`, huge numbers, or invented names.
- Address intent is weak under typos. Address questions scored only 34.8% exact.
- Kindergarten named-entity routing is weaker than school routing.
- A few personal-data requests still route to public fields instead of refusing, for example SNILS for a kindergarten head.
- Some answers hallucinate direct facts instead of calling tools or clarifying.

Conclusion: keep CLI-level deterministic guards. Do not rely on the 1.7B router alone for unsupported scopes, safety, personal data, or strict entity matching.
